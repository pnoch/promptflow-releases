Questions

Is this a subscription?

No. Both licences are one-time payments and include future updates. There is nothing to renew and nothing to cancel.

What is the difference between the two licences?

Lifetime runs two automations at a time. Lifetime Pro removes that cap. Everything else is identical.

Can I use it on more than one computer?

A licence is bound to one device. You can deactivate the current device from Settings → Licence, which frees the licence to be activated on another machine.

What do I need to run it?

Linux on x86_64 today. A Google Flow account for generation and a TikTok account for posting — both are yours, and PromptFlow drives them through your own browser session.

What happens if I am offline?

The app opens and your local data is there, but starting a new automation needs a licence check with our server. Purchases and activations also need a connection.

Refunds and receipts?
