PromptFlow Companion Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-10-28

PromptFlow Companion is a companion app for the PromptFlow desktop application. This policy explains what the app stores and transmits.

Data stored on your device

  • A scoped companion token and backend URL.
  • NAS WebDAV URL, username, and password in Android Keystore-backed encrypted storage.
  • Cached library and schedule metadata, and downloaded media.
  • App settings and notification state.

Data sent to PromptFlow servers

  • Email address when you choose email-code pairing.
  • Device name/model and a companion device identifier.
  • Allowlisted library/schedule requests, captions, and hashtags needed to provide the feature.
  • Pairing, revocation, and rate-limit metadata.

PromptFlow servers do not receive your media or NAS credentials.

Data sent to your desktop and NAS

The app sends allowlisted requests to your desktop through the PromptFlow backend. The desktop uploads media to your NAS; the phone downloads it directly with the read-only NAS account you configure.

Sharing

We do not sell your data, show ads, or include third-party analytics SDKs.

Retention and deletion

Unpairing clears the local token, cache, and downloaded media. Revoking the device on the desktop invalidates the token immediately. Contact support for backend account or device deletion.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit with HTTPS/WSS. Tokens and NAS credentials are stored with Android Keystore-backed encryption. Downloaded APKs are verified by SHA-256 before installation in the sideload build.

Children

The app is a business tool and is not directed to children under 13.
